# Reimagine outcomes

- [x] **Professional Reimagine workbench:** Provide a clean, understandable, creative and attractive UI for converting old black-and-white photos into color. Use the name “Reimagine,” remove “How it works” and extra navigation, show the full image from top to bottom without cropping, keep the workbench responsive, and prioritize natural skin-tone preservation.
- [x] **Email/password account access:** Provide open registration and sign-in for people without Manus accounts. Do not require email verification or offer email-based password recovery; do not use Manus or Google sign-in. Give each normalized email address one Reimagine account. Let signed-in users change a password by entering their current password, keep passwords hidden by default with a show/hide option, and do not display the password-recovery notice requested for removal. If a password is too short, show “Password must be at least 12 characters.” inline instead of the raw Zod issue array.
- [x] **Private per-account history:** Save each successful colorization's original and generated result to the signed-in account. Require that account on every history list, update, download and media-stream request. Never expose a stable project-storage path or signed storage URL to browser code.
- [x] **History tools and removal:** Provide library search, date sorting, favorites, photo renaming, Natural/Vivid presets, before/after comparison and downloads. Allow account-scoped history-only removal and explain at the confirmation that storage bytes are retained.
- [x] **Batch colorizing:** Accept a batch of up to five JPEG/PNG/WebP images, each no larger than 15 MB. Process sequentially, display progress/status per file, continue after a single-file failure, allow retry of failed files, and save every successful original/result as a separate item in that account's history.
- [x] **Professional notices:** Remove these notices: “No sign-in is provided. Colorizing sends your photo to AI, and project-storage links aren’t private per user. Avoid sensitive photos.” “Color and skin tones are AI estimates; review important details before keeping.” “Your original is sent to the project’s image model only when you colorize.” Replace the large notices with one concise, professional AI disclosure near Colorize; keep the sign-in, registration and account screens free of password-recovery notices; disclose soft removal only at the remove action.
- [x] **Database account/history data:** Use the approved managed Database to store unique account identities and ownership-linked photo history, and apply an additive migration without overwriting existing data.
